Our Story

Two Generations. One Standard.

Aggrepoxy didn't start in California. It started in Chicago, where the winters don't forgive a shortcut — and neither do we.

Family Owned & Operated

Built On A Chicago Standard.

My dad started this business in Chicago, where freeze-thaw winters put more stress on a concrete slab in one season than most floors see in a decade out here. In that market, a coating job that's not done right doesn't just look bad, it fails. That's the standard I grew up learning: grind it properly, use real commercial-grade material, and never take a shortcut a customer won't see until it's too late.

I brought that same standard west to build Aggrepoxy here in Southern California, where we now serve homeowners and businesses across Los Angeles, Orange County, and the Inland Empire. The climate's different. The standard isn't.

Every crew member on our team is trained to the same process: diamond-grind the slab, install commercial-grade materials, keep the job site HEPA-filtered and clean, and stand behind the work with a real, written 10-year warranty. That's not a marketing line, it's how my dad taught me to run a job, and it's how I run every one of ours.

— Jordan, Owner
