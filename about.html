Two Generations. One Standard.

Aggrepoxy didn't start in California. It started in Chicago, where the winters don't forgive a shortcut, and neither do we.

Where It Started

My dad started this business in Chicago, where freeze-thaw winters put more stress on a concrete slab in one season than most floors see in a decade out here. I grew up on his job sites, learning the trade from the ground up: how to prep a slab, mix a batch, and spot a shortcut before it became someone else's problem down the road.

That's the standard I brought west when I built Aggrepoxy here in Southern California. The climate changed. The standard didn't.

Our Promise

California floors get more sun, more foot traffic, and more scrutiny than anywhere else. Our job is to build one that holds up to all three.

Jordan Mizrahi, Owner

I grew up on job sites, not behind a desk. By the time I was a teenager I already knew how to prep a slab, mix a batch, and spot a shortcut from across the room. Freeze-thaw winters don't forgive a shortcut, and neither did my dad.

When I moved to California, I brought that same standard with me: diamond-grind the slab, use real commercial-grade material, keep the job site clean and HEPA-filtered, and back every install with a real, written warranty.

Today, Aggrepoxy is trusted by brands like Valvoline, Lexus, and McLaren, and every floor we install still gets built the way my dad taught me: the right way, or not at all.

Jordan Mizrahi, Owner
