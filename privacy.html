Privacy Policy.

Effective October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aggrepoxy Floor Coatings LLC ("Aggrepoxy," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit aggrepoxy.com or request an estimate from us.

Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you fill out an estimate or bid form, we collect your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, and any project details you choose to share. If you book an appointment through our scheduler, we also collect the information you enter there, such as your address and preferred appointment time.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our site and the services it relies on receive basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring website. We may use cookies and similar tools for analytics and advertising measurement (for example, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Google Ads, or Meta pixels) to understand how visitors use the site and how our ads perform. You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser opt-out add-on.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your request, schedule your estimate, and prepare your proposal.
  • To contact you by phone, text message, or email about your request, appointment, and project.
  • To provide our services, honor warranties, and keep business records.
  • To improve our website and measure the effectiveness of our advertising.
  • To protect against spam, fraud, and misuse of our site.

Text Messages

If you give us your phone number, we may send you text messages about your estimate request, appointment reminders, and project updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run our business:

  • Service providers that help us operate, such as our form delivery service, our scheduling and customer management software, email and text messaging providers, website hosting, and analytics providers. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Our Chicago team. Requests submitted from our Chicago page are handled by our Chicago-area team, which will contact you directly.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law, or to protect our rights, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers. If our business is sold or reorganized, customer information may transfer as part of that transaction.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to your request, complete your project, honor any warranty, and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Your Choices And Rights

  • You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and out of emails by using the unsubscribe link or contacting us.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us below. We may need to keep certain records where the law requires it.
  • You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how some parts of the site work.

California residents. California law gives you the right to request information about how we use your personal information, and to request access to or deletion of it. We do not sell personal information. Our site does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No website or online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our site and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Links To Other Sites

Our site may link to or embed services run by others, such as our appointment scheduler. Their own privacy policies apply to information you give them directly.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
