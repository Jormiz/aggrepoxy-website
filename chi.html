Where It Started

Aggrepoxy started here, in Chicago, over 10 years ago, on hands and knees grinding a garage floor. Since then it's grown into commercial and industrial work across the area, including dealership showroom installs for McLaren and Toyota and production floors for Turano Bread Company plants. That same shop trained the team now building Aggrepoxy's Southern California operation.
