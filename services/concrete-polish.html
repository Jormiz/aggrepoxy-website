Not Every Floor Needs A Coating

Sometimes The Right Answer Is The Concrete Itself.

An epoxy or polyaspartic coating is a layer built on top of your slab. Polished concrete is different — we're refining the concrete itself through a multi-step diamond grinding and honing process, progressively finer, until it's dense, smooth, and reflective.

There's no topcoat sitting on the surface to eventually wear through or need recoating. Instead we seal it with a penetrating densifier that hardens the slab from within, making it dust-proof and resistant to chemicals and abrasion — which is exactly why it's the standard for high-traffic warehouses and modern retail spaces that don't want a coated look.

Epoxy and polyaspartic systems build a coating on top of the slab. Polished concrete refines the slab itself through grinding and densifying, so there's no topcoat that can eventually peel or need recoating.
The densifying process strengthens and hardens the surface, making it more resistant to wear and cracking than untreated concrete — though like any concrete, existing structural cracks should be addressed before polishing.
