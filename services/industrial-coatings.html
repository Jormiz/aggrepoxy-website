Industrial Coating Systems — Warehouses & Distribution Facilities

Built For Forklifts. Not Just Foot Traffic.

A heavy-duty system engineered for continuous equipment loads, chemical exposure, and OSHA slip-resistance — with minimal facility downtime.

Downtime Is The Real Cost

A Floor That Fails Means A Bay Goes Down.

Warehouse floors take a different kind of abuse: forklifts, pallet jacks, constant abrasion, chemical spills, and temperature swings. Bare or under-spec'd concrete dusts, pits, and eventually cracks under that load — and every time a section needs to be redone, that's a bay or a dock you can't use.

Our Industrial System is built specifically for that punishment: a high-build polyaspartic engineered for heavy continuous loads, resistant to chemical and thermal shock, and finished to meet OSHA slip-resistance requirements. It cures fast, so you're not shutting down operations for a week to get it done.

We can also add line striping and safety markings as part of the same install, so your floor is fully operational the moment we're done.
