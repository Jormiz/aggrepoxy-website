Full-broadcast flake over a diamond-ground, commercial-grade polyaspartic base. Slip-resistant, UV-stable, installed in 1-2 days — backed by a 10-year warranty.

What's Actually Wrong With Your Garage Floor

It's Not Just Ugly. It's Getting Worse.

Bare concrete doesn't hold still. It dusts, it stains the second a quart of oil hits it, and every hairline crack you ignore turns into a bigger one under the weight of your car. A box-store epoxy kit hides it for a season, then peels at the tire line the moment you actually use the space.

That's the gap we built the Decorative Flake System to close. We diamond-grind the slab down to bare, textured concrete for a true mechanical bond, then build up a commercial-grade polyaspartic base and broadcast decorative flake to full refusal, not a light sprinkle. The result reads like a showroom floor and holds up like one.

It's the same category of system used on dealership and shop floors, sized right for a residential garage, and installed in a day or two instead of a week.
