Our Install Process

The AGX Standard

No shortcuts, no mystery. Here's exactly how we build your floor.

01Grind

We diamond-grind the slab to open the surface for a true mechanical bond.

02Repair

Cracks, chips, and pitting are filled and ground flush.

03Coat

A high-solids basecoat is rolled on and bonds tight to the concrete.

04Broadcast

Your chosen blend is distributed into the wet basecoat for full coverage.

05Clean

Flake floors are scraped smooth. Quartz floors are swept clean.

06Seal

A UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat locks it all in. No yellowing, no peeling.
