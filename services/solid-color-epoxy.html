Solid Color Epoxy — Retail, Commercial & Multi-Unit Properties

One Consistent Floor, Wall To Wall.

A uniform, seamless, mop-and-go surface in dozens of standard or custom colors — built for spaces that need to look sharp on day one and day one thousand.

A Patchwork Floor Says Something You Don't Want It To

Mismatched Concrete Looks Unfinished. Even When It Isn't.

Multiple tenants, phased buildouts, or years of patch repairs leave a lot of commercial floors looking inconsistent, even when the space itself is in good shape. It's hard to keep clean, it doesn't photograph well for listings, and it quietly undercuts what you can charge for the space.

Solid Color Epoxy solves that with one continuous, seamless surface from wall to wall — no seams, no seams to catch dirt, and no patchwork of old and new concrete. We offer dozens of standard colors plus custom matching, with a satin or gloss topcoat depending on the look you want.

It's the same diamond-ground, commercial-grade base used across every Aggrepoxy system, just built for uniformity instead of texture.
