Both start with a diamond grind. Sealed concrete is finished with a protective sealer in matte or gloss. Polished concrete goes through multiple finer diamond passes until the concrete itself shines. Polished costs more, but lasts longer and needs less upkeep.
When dry, it's no more slippery than most smooth floors. Like tile or stone, it can get slick when wet, so for wet areas we'll recommend a lower sheen or an anti-slip treatment.
Polished concrete can last decades with basic care, because the finish is the slab itself. Sealed floors last for years and can be resealed when they start to show wear.
