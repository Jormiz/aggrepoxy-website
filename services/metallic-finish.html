Your Product Deserves A Floor That Matches It.

A customer decides how they feel about your space before they've read a single price tag. Flat gray epoxy or dated tile does the product no favors — and by now, your customers have seen it at the shop down the street too.

Our Metallic Finish system is hand-applied, which means the pigment moves and pools differently every time — a true 3D, marbled look that can't be copied from floor to floor. We color-match it to your brand, build it over the same commercial-grade base used in our industrial systems, and cap it with a mirror-gloss topcoat that wipes clean.

It's a floor that photographs well, holds up to daily foot and cart traffic, and gives people a reason to stop and look down.
