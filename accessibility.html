Accessibility.

Last reviewed October 10, 2026

Aggrepoxy Floor Coatings LLC wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use aggrepoxy.com, learn about our floor coating services, and request an estimate.

Our Standard

We aim for this website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. We work toward that by building accessibility into the site's code rather than relying on a plug-in or overlay.

What We Have Done

  • Text and interface colors checked against WCAG contrast requirements
  • Full keyboard access, including a "skip to main content" link, visible focus indicators, and a keyboard-operable before/after photo slider
  • Labeled form fields with written error messages that are announced to screen readers
  • Pause controls for slideshows and video, and reduced animation for visitors who turn on "reduce motion" in their device settings
  • Descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images and a consistent page structure with headings and landmarks

Known Limitations

Our estimate scheduler is provided by a third-party service (DripJobs) and opens in a pop-up window. We do not control its accessibility. If you have trouble using it, call or email us and we will schedule your estimate directly.

Feedback and Help

If you run into a barrier on this site, or need information in a different format, please tell us. We will do our best to respond within two business days and provide the information or service another way.
