Yes. Every estimate is a free, no-obligation on-site walkthrough. We'll look at your slab, talk through your goals, and can usually give you a firm number before we leave.
Cost depends on square footage, the current condition of your slab, and which system you choose (flake, solid color, or metallic). There's no accurate way to quote a floor sight-unseen, which is why the walkthrough is free.
Most residential garages are installed in 1 to 2 days, from diamond grinding to the final topcoat. Larger industrial and commercial floors are scoped individually based on square footage and your operating schedule.
Timing depends on the system installed and the weather during cure. We'll walk you through exact foot-traffic and vehicle-traffic timelines for your specific floor before we leave the job site.
Yes. Every install is backed by a 10-Year Warranty. If an issue ever stems from our product or workmanship, our team will step in and make it right.
Our warranty covers our product and workmanship. Cracking caused by slab movement or settling that's already present, or that happens after installation, isn't something a surface coating can prevent, but we inspect your slab during the estimate and flag any concerns up front.
